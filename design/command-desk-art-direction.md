## Production formula (used for this three-image set)

Historically grounded editorial game illustration in restrained oil and gouache,
with deliberate brushwork and clear architectural silhouettes. Environments use
charcoal blue and muted olive; people and focal documents carry warm ivory and
subdued brass, while warning red is reserved for interface signals, not decorative
lighting. Directional practical light creates a sober, human atmosphere without
cinematic haze or glossy surfaces. Maintain readable subject separation and a
consistent eye-level three-quarter perspective, leaving quiet negative space for
adjacent interface text.

This exact paragraph was combined with each scene and the common restrictions in
`higgsfield-command-desk-production.md`. For future eras, review a small pilot set
before extending production; do not silently charge for repeated generations.

## Interface rules

- Keep the existing blue-gray shell, muted brass selection and stable navigation.
- One main decision document; remaining decisions are list rows, not equal cards.
- Mission checklist and recent confirmed records remain visible beside the work.
- Header identifies the actual office. Authority is a compact readable band.
- Status cannot rely on color alone. Costs and denial reasons remain visible.
- Political recognition and local administration are independent, never a fake
  sequential wizard. Record selection must not execute an action.
- Reading, art loading, hovering or opening a panel never grants a reward.
- Copy, controls and numbers are real HTML. No image-embedded labels or buttons.
- Real map geometry stays data-driven; illustrations never locate units/borders.
- No fake named historical portraits, fake event outcomes or invented source credits.
- No permanent background video. Any later narrative clip needs skip/reduced motion.
- Typography is role-specific: office title, decision title, body, evidence and
  tabular numeric data. Do not add a decorative font for long Korean body copy.
- Max reading width is bounded on 4K. Additional space is not longer paragraphs.
- CSS is scoped to the owning view; no new global card or heading reset.

## First asset gate

Use the native 2048x1360 sources without downscaling or upscaling. The original
640x426 HTML slot is a display ratio, not a source-resolution cap. Render as
small supporting figures, never as the entire UI. Label them as atmosphere, not
actual event records. Display only for 1936-1959; omit on an unknown/out-of-range
date rather than depict 2060 using wartime equipment. The branch is a presentation
choice, not a new source of RNG, save state or achievement unlocks.

Later candidates at the first gate: postwar meeting rooms, modern situation rooms,
paired city damage/reconstruction art and event-specific newspaper illustrations.
The first two are now implemented by the broad extension below; damage/recovery
pairs and event-specific documentary illustrations remain outside this set.
Do not substitute another generation provider without disclosing the change.
